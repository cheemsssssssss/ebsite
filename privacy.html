隐私政策 (Privacy Policy)

更新日期：2026 年 8 月 适用范围：IE LaTeX 公式编辑器全站服务 遵循原则：数据最小化 / 纯本地计算 / 学术隐私保护

核心承诺摘要： IE LaTeX 公式编辑器高度重视您的数据隐私与学术知识产权。本站采用纯前端本地计算架构，您在输入框中编辑的所有数学公式、推导草稿均在您的本地浏览器中实时渲染，绝不向任何第三方云端服务器上传或保存您的公式内容。

1. 信息收集与使用原则

当您访问和使用 IE LaTeX 公式编辑器时，我们遵循“最少必要”原则，不强制要求注册账户，亦不收集您的真实姓名、手机号等敏感个人信息。

  • 浏览器本地存储（Local Storage）： 用于在您的设备本地保存您的公式历史记录（方便您找回最近编辑的内容）、深浅色主题设置以及字号缩放偏好。您可以随时通过浏览器设置或点击“清空历史”彻底删除。
  • 常规网络日志（Log Data）： 基础技术信息包括 IP 地址、浏览器类型与访问时间戳，仅用于防范恶意滥用与服务安全监控。

2. 公式数据与学术隐私安全

排版内核基于 KaTeX 和 MathJax 前端脚本执行：所有 LaTeX 代码解析、MathML 生成、SVG 矢量化与图片光栅化全在您的本机设备中瞬时完成。我们不会在云端建立任何数据库记录您的公式，保障学术未公开论文与试题绝对保密。

3. 联系我们与隐私权利主张

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问或希望行使隐私权利，请联系我们的团队：coco@itcude.com。

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 Scope: IE LaTeX Formula Studio Principles: Data Minimization / 100% Client-Side Privacy / Academic Protection

Core Commitment: The IE LaTeX Formula Editor is committed to protecting your privacy and intellectual property. We use a 100% client-side computing architecture. Every formula you type or render is processed entirely within your local browser sandbox. Your formulas are never uploaded to or stored on any external server.

1. Information Collection & Usage

We respect the principle of data minimization. You can use our editor freely without creating an account or providing sensitive personal information.

  • Browser Local Storage: We use HTML5 LocalStorage to remember your recent formula history, dark/light theme preferences, and editor height. This data never leaves your device and can be deleted at any time via your browser settings or the "Clear History" button.
  • Standard Web Server Logs: Basic technical logs such as IP address, browser type, and timestamps are collected solely for cybersecurity maintenance and network stability.

2. Strict Formula Confidentiality & Academic Privacy

Because our parsing and rendering engines (KaTeX and MathJax) run client-side in your browser's JavaScript runtime, all equation syntax analysis, MathML generation, and PNG rasterizations occur locally. Your unpublished research drafts and examination questions remain strictly confidential.

3. Contact Us for Privacy Inquiries

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us at: coco@itcude.com.
